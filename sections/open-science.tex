Several targeted initiatives have been undertaken to foster and strengthen the adoption of open science practices within the research community.

First of all, a significant initiative was the organization, in September 2026, of an advanced training school on Open Data 
(the second school of this type over the duration of EURO-LABS). The school aimed to equip the next generation of stakeholders, including PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, research staff, and data managers, with knowledge and skills related to open science and research data management. 
The organized lectures and activities provided hands-on training
in FAIR data curation, metadata standards, and tooling for reproducible research.

Notably, the initiative of this kind of training was conceived during the course of the EURO-LABS project, emerging from the collaborative exchanges and interactions among project partners.

Concerning data treatment, 
the open-access repository on Zenodo, available at https://zenodo.org/ communities/euro-labs/records, serves as a central platform for collecting and disseminating project outputs. Digital resources, including deliverables, presentations, posters, and source codes, are regularly uploaded, in compliance with the data management guidelines defined under Work Package 5 (WP5.2).

Furthermore, within WP5, 
the openNP catalogue, providing
researchers with a user-friendly, metadata-rich repository for experimental datasets in nuclear physics,
was successfully launched, and 
different data lake prototypes are being developed/tested.
An Authorization and Authentication Infrastructure (AAI) service, based on the IAM solution, was developed and made available. This infrastructure has been successfully integrated into several services, both within the EURO-LABS project, such as Theo4Exp, which provides computational virtual access (VA), and the OpenNP catalogue, and in external environments. Examples include authentication services for logging and graphing systems at GANIL, as well as collaborative platforms at GSI/FAIR.


Further information on the project's open science activities is provided in the descriptions of the individual WP5 tasks.
