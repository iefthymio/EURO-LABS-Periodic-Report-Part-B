Following the roadmap outlined in the previous reporting period, the budget reallocation strategy was formally presented to and validated by the Governing Board during the fourth EURO-LABS Annual Meeting (FAME), held in Ljubljana in early October 2025. While an initial tranche of budget adjustments was approved at that meeting, the Scientific Coordinator subsequently requested an additional round of refinements to capture and redistribute further portions of budget that would otherwise have remained unspent by certain beneficiaries.
 
The reallocation followed a structured, bottom-up assessment across the consortium. Each WP leader consulted their respective facility coordinators to forecast actual financial needs for the remaining months of the project. Facilities experiencing lower-than-expected demand or operational difficulties in delivering Transnational Access (TA) released their unspent allocations back to the consortium, allowing these funds to be redirected toward high-demand facilities. In parallel, the leaders of WP1 (Management) and WP5 (Dissemination) evaluated their remaining budgets against actual operational requirements to ensure full alignment with project needs.
 
Overall, this comprehensive redistribution involved 16 out of the 25 project beneficiaries and resulted in the internal reallocation of a total of EUR 1,103,206.00 across the consortium. Once finalized, the consolidated redistribution plan was submitted to the European Commission to be formalized through a Grant Agreement Amendment. Although the administrative completion of the amendment was delayed by the intervening need to incorporate the legal transformation and PIC change of ATOMKI (transitioning from BEN-17 to BEN-34), the procedure was successfully brought to completion, ensuring the most efficient utilization of the project's financial resources.
